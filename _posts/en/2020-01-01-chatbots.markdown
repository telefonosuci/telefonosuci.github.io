Chatbots have existed in various forms since 1966 (ELIZA). Only recently have companies learned to use them for customer support, sales, payment collection and social media engagement. Are they only for big businesses with large budgets, or should small businesses consider them too?

### What is a chatbot?

A chatbot is software that simulates a conversation. It lives in apps, live chat, email, SMS and messaging platforms such as WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook Messenger and Slack, and it often connects to a CRM to capture leads and customer data. It follows predefined rules, frequently combined with artificial intelligence to handle questions that a fixed script can't.

### Why consider one for a small business?

- **24/7 service**: a chatbot never sleeps or takes holidays.
- **Instant answers to simple questions**: no waiting for an operator, a call or an email.
- **Efficiency**: routine requests are handled automatically, freeing sales and support for valuable conversations.
- **Consistent experience**: a chatbot doesn't get stressed by difficult customers, and with AI it can personalise answers based on past conversations.
- **Customers like them**: more and more people look for answers online, on their own.
- **Data**: every conversation tells you something about what your customers need.

### The best use cases

- **Routing**: a few questions to send the customer to the right department.
- **Support**: suggesting knowledge base articles that solve the problem.
- **Orders**: guiding the customer through the purchase directly in the chat.
- **Product advice**: a few questions to recommend the right product.
- **Payments**: integrating a payment system into the conversation.
- **Social**: increasing engagement and sending people to the right page of the site.

Map the customer journey first: it will show you where a chatbot actually saves time. And always leave an easy way to talk to a person: a chatbot that traps the customer does more harm than no chatbot.

### Real examples

- **Buoy**: a health chatbot that asks questions about symptoms and suggests possible next steps, based on thousands of clinical papers.
- **Next Insurance**: an insurer for small businesses that offers quotes and policies via Facebook Messenger.
- **HelloVote**: a non-profit chatbot that simplifies voter registration via SMS and Messenger.

## How it works

- **three.js, loaded only here**: the rest of the site doesn't download a single byte of 3D. glTF models come later, only if they exist; until then everything is made of plain boxes (greybox) and the game is already playable.
- **One state machine, one system per phase**: filling, folding, taping and shipping are separate modules that get the pointer only when it's their turn. Scoring rules are pure functions, easy to tune.
- **HTML before canvas**: instructions and results are HTML generated by Jekyll, readable without JavaScript. The game pauses when it scrolls off screen and, under `prefers-reduced-motion`, skips animations without breaking.

It's a work in progress: drag & drop, tape folding over the edges, the shipping label and real models are on the way.
